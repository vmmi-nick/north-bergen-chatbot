Welcome

This is a simple demonstration page showing how the North Bergen Constituent Chatbot appears when it is added to a township webpage. The chat panel opens on its own a moment after the page loads. If you close it, it folds down to the round chat button in the corner, and it stays closed while you move around the site.

Try asking it when taxes are due, when the next commissioners meeting is, or use the Report an issue button inside the chat to prepare a report to call in.
